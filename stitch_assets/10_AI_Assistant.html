The Android Activity Lifecycle

The lifecycle consists of several stages that an Activity transitions through. Here is a high-level breakdown:

play_circle

Creation Phase

  • • onCreate(): App startup logic.
  • • onStart(): Activity becomes visible.
  • • onResume(): Foreground interaction.
stop_circle

Destruction Phase

  • • onPause(): Partially hidden.
  • • onStop(): Fully hidden.
  • • onDestroy(): Cleanup before closing.

Tip: Always use onPause() or onStop() to release heavy resources like location listeners or animations.
